## 1. WeChat Official Account Cover / Article Header Image

**Specifications**:
- Cover Image: 2.35:1 (900×383px or 1200×510px)
- Body Illustration: 16:9 (1200×675px) or 4:3 (1200×900px)

**Key Design Elements**:
- Prioritize visual impact (users quickly scan in information feeds)
- Minimal or no text (WeChat official account title will overlay it)

## 4. PPT / Keynote Presentation

**Specifications**:
- Standard: 16:9 (1920×1080px)
- Widescreen: 16:10 (1920×1200px)

**Key Design Elements**:
- One core message per slide (no clutter)
- Clear font size hierarchy (title 40pt+ / body 24pt+ / notes 16pt+)
- Generous whitespace for clearer projection
- Image-to-text ratio at least 60:40
- Consistent visual system (colors, fonts, spacing)
