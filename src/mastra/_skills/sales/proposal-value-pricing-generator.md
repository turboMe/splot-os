# Procedure: B2B Value-Based Proposal & 3-Tier Pricing Generator

Use this procedure to turn discovery notes into a compelling, executive-level commercial proposal structured to maximize deal closing rates and average contract value (ACV).

---

## 1. Proposal Architecture

1. **Executive Summary & Stated Problem:**
   - Frame the proposal strictly around the client's quantifiable pain (e.g., *"Losing 25h/week to manual data entry costing ~120,000 zł/year"*).

2. **The 3-Tier Pricing Strategy (Good - Better - Best):**
   - **Tier 1 (Foundation):** Solves the core immediate bottleneck. Minimum viable scope.
   - **Tier 2 (Growth / Recommended):** The optimal solution. Includes automated pipelines, training, and ongoing support. (Anchor target tier).
   - **Tier 3 (Enterprise / Transformation):** Complete white-glove migration, custom integrations, SLA guarantees, and priority support.

3. **ROI & Payback Period:**
   - Quantify financial ROI: *"At 4,500 zł/mo investment, breakeven is achieved in 45 days based on 15h weekly time savings"*.

4. **Risk Reversal & Guarantee:**
   - Include a milestone-based acceptance guarantee or a 30-day proof-of-concept clause.
