# Procedure: High-Retention Hook & Storytelling Framework

Use this procedure to craft engaging thought leadership posts, LinkedIn articles, or X/Twitter threads that maximize read-through rates and engagement.

---

## 1. The 3-Beat Hook Architecture

1. **The Hook (First 2 Lines):**
   - Must disrupt expectations, state a contrarian observation, or present a surprising metric.
   - Avoid generic openers (*"I'm excited to share..."*, *"In today's fast world..."*).
   - Hook types: *The Paradox*, *The Mistake Breakdown*, *The Data Discrepancy*, *The Step-by-Step System*.

2. **The Body (Problem -> Agitate -> Reframe -> System):**
   - Use 1-sentence paragraphs with rhythmic line breaks.
   - Ground insights in concrete real-world numbers, architectures, or case studies.
   - Provide an actionable mental model or diagram.

3. **The Payoff & Discussion Prompt:**
   - Summarize the core takeaway in 3 concise bullet points.
   - End with an open-ended, non-trivial discussion question to drive high-signal comments.
